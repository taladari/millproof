# Outreach

Everything here is asynchronous. No calls are offered, because none are needed and because a
calendar in Israel does not survive US business hours.

Rules that apply to every message below:

- Never assert what someone's duty exposure is. You do not know their tariff position.
- Never classify their goods, even casually. See `compliance-boundary.md`.
- Lead with the deadline and the data gap, not with the product.
- One follow-up, then stop.

## 5. What not to send

- No "quick 15 minutes?" - a call is not the ask, and it breaks the async constraint.
- No free audits of their entries. That is customs business and it is a services treadmill.
- No claims about penalty amounts or their specific exposure.
- No cold email to scraped addresses.
