# Outreach targets

Built 2026-07-28. Two audiences, two different asks, both asynchronous.

**Brokers and forwarders** publish CSMS explainers to their own importer mailing lists. They are
not building tools and they need content. Ask: use this free check with your readers. Template 3
in `outreach.md`.

**Trade law firms and advisory blogs** publish the same bulletins with legal analysis. They will
not promote a vendor, but they do cite sources, and a citation from one of them is worth more
than any ad. Ask: here is a free tool that answers the operational question your alert raises,
cite it if it helps.

`verified` means I loaded a real 232 or CSMS post from them during research on 2026-07-26/28.
Unverified rows are plausible from reputation and need 60 seconds of checking before you send.

## Sending discipline

- One at a time, from `hello@millproof.com`, written per recipient. No mail merge, no bulk send.
- Split across two days if your provider's daily send cap is low.
- Log every send in `tracking.csv` with `channel=broker` or `channel=lawfirm`.
- Never claim their readers are non-compliant. Offer the tool, name the bulletin, stop.
